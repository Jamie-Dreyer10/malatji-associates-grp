About us

One trusted partner. Integrated expertise.

A multidisciplinary professional consulting firm delivering innovative and sustainable solutions across the built environment.

Who we are

Our role

Malatji and Associates is your trusted and leading consulting and strategic partner in infrastructure development and management, providing turnkey and intuitive engineering that brings about sound and buildable solutions.

We provide planning, design, engineering, project management and strategic advisory services. Our services extend to Engineering, Procurement, Construction and Management (EPCM) support, helping clients plan, execute and optimise complex projects throughout their lifecycle.

Our team

Skills under one roof

Our multidisciplinary team brings together engineers, architects, project managers, strategic advisors, technicians, analysts, quantity surveyors, health and safety professionals, environmental specialists and construction management experts.

Together we serve as a single, reliable interlocutor for our clients.

Our value proposition

One trusted partner. Integrated expertise. Sustainable solutions. Exceptional project delivery.

We are committed to excellence, innovation, technical expertise and sustainable outcomes that create long-term value for clients, stakeholders and communities.
